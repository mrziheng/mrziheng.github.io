E-RAM is a geospatial resource assessment workflow for power-system planning. It converts meteorological, land-cover, terrain, hydrological, biomass, and geological inputs into model-ready resource potential datasets for long-term energy-system optimization.

## Modules

- Wind power: hourly capacity factors and installable capacity for onshore and offshore wind.
- Solar PV: utility-scale and distributed rooftop PV potential.
- Hydropower and pumped storage: dam-site and reservoir-level technical-economic screening.
- Biomass: agricultural, forestry, and grassland residue resources.
- Carbon storage: deep saline aquifer storage potential for CCS and BECCS analysis.

## Outputs

The workflow produces gridded capacity potential, hourly capacity-factor profiles, annual generation potential, and storage or carbon-sequestration potential in formats such as NetCDF, GeoTIFF, and CSV. These datasets support RESPO, CISPO, and GISPO model runs.
