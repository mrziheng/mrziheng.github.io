CISPO is a China-focused power-system planning model for studying deep decarbonization pathways. It combines spatially resolved renewable energy siting, hourly system operation, storage and transmission expansion, and firm low-carbon resources to evaluate feasible pathways toward carbon neutrality.

Recent applications include China's 2060 power-system transition, pumped-storage and hydropower flexibility, carbon capture and storage, land-use constraints, and the economics of renewable-dominated electricity markets.

## Research Uses

- Long-term transition pathways for China's power sector.
- Site-level and grid-level coordination of renewable energy, storage, hydropower, and transmission.
- Capacity adequacy, market revenue sufficiency, and policy design under high renewable penetration.
